G-SOFT Backup

Automatic Google Drive backup for G-SOFT business software, made by G-SOFT, Sri Lanka.

G-SOFT makes point-of-sale and business software for shops, such as G-SOFT Inventory Management System and G-SOFT Inventory Manager. These programs keep the shop's records (products, sales, customers and so on) in a database on the shop's own computer.

G-SOFT Backup is the Google Drive feature inside these programs. When the shop owner connects their own Google account, the program regularly uploads a backup of the shop's database to that Google Drive, so the records are safe even if the computer is lost, stolen or broken.

How it works

  • The shop owner signs in with Google from inside the program, and the backups go into their own Google Drive.
  • Each backup is locked (encrypted) with a password chosen by the shop before it leaves the computer.
  • The program only uploads, lists, downloads and deletes the backup files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in the Google Drive.
  • Old backups are deleted automatically, so only the newest ones are kept.
  • A backup can be restored from Google Drive, including onto a new computer.
G-SOFT does not receive, store or see the shop's data. The backups go straight from the shop's computer to the shop's own Google Drive. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
