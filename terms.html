The service

G-SOFT Backup copies your G-SOFT program's database to your own Google Drive and lets you restore it from there. It runs on your computer. Your Google account and Google Drive are provided by Google and are covered by Google's own terms.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep your backup password safe. Backups can't be restored without it, and G-SOFT cannot recover a lost password.
  • Keep enough free space in your Google Drive for the backups.
  • Check from time to time that backups are working (the program shows the last good backup).
  • Restoring a backup replaces all current data in the program with the data in that backup. Only restore when you mean to.
  • We recommend keeping other backups too, for example on a USB drive.

Stopping

You can stop using G-SOFT Backup at any time by clicking Disconnect in the program. Backups already in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them.
