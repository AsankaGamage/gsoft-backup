Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what the Google Drive backup feature ("G-SOFT Backup") in G-SOFT software does with your information. G-SOFT Backup runs on your own computer as part of a G-SOFT program, such as G-SOFT Inventory Management System.

What Google information the app uses

When you connect a Google account, the app asks Google for two permissions only:

  • Your e-mail address (userinfo.email). This is used only to show which Google account is connected.
  • Files created by this app in your Google Drive (drive.file). This lets the app create a "G-SOFT Inventory Backups" folder and upload, list, download and delete the backup files it made itself. The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

How the information is used

  • Backups of your business database are encrypted on your computer with a password you choose, then uploaded to your own Google Drive.
  • When you restore, the app downloads a backup you pick, unlocks it with your password and puts it back into your database.
  • Old backups are deleted from your Google Drive automatically, keeping the number you choose.

Where your information is kept

  • Your backups are kept only in your own Google Drive.
  • The Google sign-in and your backup password are kept only on your computer, protected by Windows for your Windows user account.
  • Nothing is sent to G-SOFT. G-SOFT has no server that receives your data. G-SOFT cannot see your Google account, your Google Drive or your backups.
  • Because the backups are encrypted with your password, they can't be opened without it, by G-SOFT or by anyone else.

Sharing

G-SOFT does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Google API Services User Data Policy

G-SOFT Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • To stop backups and remove the app's access, click Disconnect in the program (Backup & Restore → Google Drive Backup), or remove "G-SOFT Backup" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the backups, delete the "G-SOFT Inventory Backups" folder from your Google Drive.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
